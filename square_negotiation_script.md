## The Negotiation Script

### Phase 1: Opening with Enthusiasm and Gratitude

**"Thank you so much for extending the offer for the Senior Software Engineer position at Square. I'm genuinely excited about the opportunity to contribute to Square's mission of economic empowerment and to work with such an innovative team. The role aligns perfectly with my career goals and technical expertise in Python development."**

### Phase 2: Acknowledging the Offer

**"I've had a chance to review the offer details, and I really appreciate the comprehensive package you've put together. It's clear that Square values its engineering talent, and I'm eager to discuss how we can arrive at a compensation structure that reflects both my experience and the value I'll bring to the team."**

### Phase 3: Presenting Market Research (Choose appropriate option)

**Option A - Direct Market Reference:**
**"Based on my research of current market conditions for Senior Software Engineers with Python expertise, I've found that similar roles typically range from $243,000 to $340,000 in total compensation, with a median around $284,000. Given my [X years] of experience and proven track record in [specific achievements], I believe my qualifications position me competitively within this range."**

**Option B - Softer Approach:**
**"I've done some research on industry standards for this role and level of experience. While I understand every company has its own compensation philosophy, I'd love to discuss whether there's flexibility to align the offer more closely with current market rates for senior Python engineers with my background."**

### Phase 4: Making Your Counter-Proposal

**Salary Negotiation:**
**"Considering my background and the industry benchmarks, I was hoping we could discuss adjusting the base salary to [specific amount or range]. This would better reflect the experience and expertise I'm bringing to the role."**

**Total Compensation Approach:**
**"Looking at the total compensation package, I'd like to explore whether we can reach [target total comp amount]. I'm flexible on how this is structured between base salary, equity, and other components."**

### Phase 5: Exploring Alternative Compensation Elements

**"If there are constraints around the base salary, I'd be very open to discussing other components of the package. For example:**
- **Additional equity/stock options**
- **A signing bonus to bridge any gap**
- **Enhanced professional development budget**
- **Flexible work arrangements or additional PTO**
- **Earlier equity vesting schedule**
- **Performance bonus opportunities"**

### Phase 6: Demonstrating Flexibility and Collaboration

**"I want to emphasize that I'm committed to finding a solution that works for both of us. I understand that compensation decisions involve multiple factors, and I'm open to creative approaches that align with Square's compensation philosophy while recognizing the value I bring."**

### Phase 7: Reinforcing Your Interest

**"Regardless of where we land on compensation, I want to reiterate my strong interest in this role. Square's impact on financial inclusion and the technical challenges you're solving really resonate with me. I'm confident that my Python expertise and [specific relevant skills] will allow me to make immediate contributions to the team."**

### Phase 8: Next Steps and Timeline

**"I'd love to hear your thoughts on this. What's the best way to move forward with these discussions? I'm hoping we can work through this relatively quickly, as I'm excited to potentially start contributing to Square's mission."**

## Follow-Up Strategies

### If They Need Time to Consider:
**"I completely understand that you may need to review this internally. Would it be helpful if I provided any additional information about my background or specific examples of my work? I'm happy to wait for your response and appreciate you taking the time to consider my request."**

### If Initial Response is Negative:
**"I appreciate you getting back to me on this. While I understand there may be constraints on [specific component], I'm wondering if we could explore other aspects of the package. My goal is to find a structure that works for both Square and reflects the value I'll bring to the team."**

### If They Agree to Some Adjustments:
**"This is fantastic, thank you for working with me on this. I really appreciate Square's flexibility and willingness to find a mutually beneficial arrangement. This reinforces my excitement about joining the team and contributing to the company's success."**

---

## Backup Negotiation Points

### Non-Monetary Value Adds:
1. **Professional Development**: Conference attendance, training budget, certification support
2. **Work-Life Balance**: Flexible hours, remote work options, additional PTO
3. **Career Growth**: Clear promotion timeline, mentorship opportunities, leadership training
4. **Technology**: Equipment allowance, home office setup, latest tools/software

### If Negotiation Stalls:
**"I understand there may be limitations on certain aspects of the offer. Rather than going back and forth extensively, would it be helpful to schedule a brief call to discuss this in real-time? I'm confident we can find a solution that works for everyone."**

---

## Key Principles to Remember

1. **Always lead with enthusiasm** for the role and company
2. **Use collaborative language** ("we," "us," "together")
3. **Be specific** with numbers and requests
4. **Show flexibility** and openness to creative solutions
5. **Emphasize mutual benefit** rather than just your needs
6. **Maintain professionalism** throughout the process
7. **Be prepared to walk away** if the offer doesn't meet your minimum requirements (but don't threaten this unless you mean it)
