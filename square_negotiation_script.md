## Opening Script

### Initial Response (24-48 hours after receiving offer)
```
"Thank you so much for the offer and for the time you've invested in getting to know me throughout this process. I'm genuinely excited about the opportunity to join The Square team and contribute to [specific project/team/initiative you discussed].

I'd like to take some time to review the offer details thoroughly and discuss a few aspects of the compensation package. Would you be available for a brief call [specific day/time] to discuss this further?"
```

## Main Negotiation Script

### 1. Express Enthusiasm & Value Alignment
```
"I'm really excited about this opportunity at Square. The work you're doing with [specific product/technology] aligns perfectly with my experience in [relevant skills], and I believe I can make significant contributions to [specific goals discussed during interviews].

During our conversations, I was particularly impressed by [specific aspect of company culture/mission/team] and I'm confident I can help drive [specific outcomes]."
```

### 2. Acknowledge the Offer Positively
```
"I appreciate the offer you've extended. The base salary of [current offer] and the equity package show that Square values engineering talent. I'm particularly excited about the [specific benefits/perks] that were mentioned."
```

### 3. Present Your Case for Higher Compensation
```
"Based on my research and experience, I'd like to discuss the compensation package. I bring [X] years of experience in [specific technologies], including [specific achievements/projects], and I've consistently delivered [specific results] in my previous roles.

Given the market rates for senior Python developers in this area and my specific expertise in [relevant skills], I was hoping we could discuss adjusting the base salary to [target range: $X-$Y] and potentially revisiting the equity component."
```

### 4. Specific Requests (Choose 2-3 to focus on)

#### Base Salary Negotiation
```
"I understand Square's compensation philosophy, and I'm wondering if there's flexibility in the base salary. Given my experience with [specific skills] and the market rates for senior developers, I was hoping we could discuss a base salary in the range of [specific number]."
```

#### Equity Negotiation
```
"I'm also interested in understanding the equity structure. Could you help me understand the vesting schedule and whether there's room to adjust the equity component given my experience level?"
```

#### Signing Bonus
```
"I was also wondering if there's a signing bonus component that could help with the transition, particularly given the [specific circumstances: relocation, leaving unvested equity, etc.]."
```

### 5. Demonstrate Flexibility
```
"I'm flexible on the specific structure - whether it's a higher base salary, additional equity, or a signing bonus. My priority is ensuring this is a mutually beneficial arrangement that reflects the value I can bring to Square."
```

---

## Response to Counteroffers

### If They Increase the Offer
```
"Thank you for the revised offer. I appreciate you taking the time to reconsider. This is definitely moving in the right direction. Let me review the details and get back to you within [timeframe]."
```

### If They Push Back on Salary
```
"I understand Square has established compensation bands. Could you help me understand the range for this role and what factors determine where someone falls within that range? I want to make sure I'm being evaluated fairly given my experience and skills."
```

### If They Say "This is Our Best Offer"
```
"I appreciate you being direct about that. Could you help me understand what opportunities there might be for growth and advancement once I'm in the role? I want to make sure this is a long-term fit for both of us."
```

## Closing Script

### Accepting the Offer
```
"Thank you for working with me on this. I'm excited to accept the offer and join the Square team. I'm confident this will be a great partnership, and I'm looking forward to contributing to [specific goals/projects]."
```

### If Accepting with Reservations
```
"I appreciate the time you've invested in this process. While the compensation isn't exactly where I had hoped, I'm excited about the opportunity and the potential for growth at Square. I'm ready to accept the offer and get started."
```

## Key Negotiation Principles

### Do's
- ✅ Express genuine enthusiasm for the role and company
- ✅ Focus on your value and specific contributions
- ✅ Be prepared with market data and your achievements
- ✅ Ask questions to understand their constraints
- ✅ Show flexibility in how compensation is structured
- ✅ Maintain a collaborative, not adversarial tone

### Don'ts
- ❌ Don't make ultimatums or threats
- ❌ Don't compare yourself to other candidates
- ❌ Don't focus only on money - mention growth opportunities
- ❌ Don't rush the process - take time to consider counteroffers
- ❌ Don't burn bridges if you need to decline

---

## Follow-up Actions

### After Accepting
1. **Written Confirmation**: Send a brief email confirming acceptance
2. **Transition Planning**: Discuss start date and onboarding
3. **Relationship Building**: Connect with future team members on LinkedIn

### If Declining
```
"Thank you for the opportunity and for the time you've invested in this process. After careful consideration, I've decided to pursue a different opportunity. I have great respect for Square and the team, and I appreciate the offer you extended."
```
