## Negotiation Strategy Variations

### If You Have Competing Offers
```
"I have another offer from [Company] that's quite competitive. However, I'm particularly excited about Square's mission and the technical challenges here. I'm hoping we can find a way to make this work financially."
```

### If You're Currently Underpaid
```
"In my current role, I've been focused on growth and learning rather than compensation. Now that I'm making a move, I want to ensure I'm being compensated at market rates for my experience and skills."
```

### If You Have Unique Skills
```
"My experience with [specific technology/skill] is particularly relevant to Square's work on [specific project]. This expertise typically commands a premium in the market, and I believe it will be valuable to the team."
```

## Common Square Responses & Counter-Strategies

### "We have established salary bands"
**Response**: "I understand Square has structured compensation. Could you help me understand where I fall within the band for this role and what factors determine placement?"

### "This is our standard offer for this level"
**Response**: "I appreciate that. Given my specific experience with [relevant skills] and the market rates, I was hoping we could discuss how my background might warrant consideration for the higher end of the range."

### "We can't increase the base salary"
**Response**: "I understand there may be constraints on base salary. Are there other ways we could structure the compensation, such as additional equity, a signing bonus, or accelerated vesting?"

## Equity Negotiation Points

- **Vesting Schedule**: Typically 4 years with 1-year cliff

## Timeline and Follow-up Strategy

### Week 1: Initial Response
- Send thank you email within 24 hours
- Request time to review and discuss
- Schedule negotiation call

### Week 2: Negotiation
- Conduct main negotiation call
- Present your case professionally
- Listen to their constraints and concerns
- Ask for time to consider counteroffers

### Week 3: Decision
- Review all offers and counteroffers
- Make final decision
- Communicate clearly and professionally
- Begin transition planning

## Red Flags to Watch For

### Concerning Responses
- **Pressure tactics**: "This offer expires in 24 hours"
- **Dismissive attitude**: "Take it or leave it"
- **Unwillingness to discuss**: "This is non-negotiable"
- **Vague promises**: "We'll review compensation later"

### How to Handle Red Flags
```
"I appreciate the offer, but I need time to make a thoughtful decision. Rushing this process wouldn't be good for either of us."
```

## Success Metrics

### What Success Looks Like
- **Compensation**: 10-20% increase from initial offer
- **Relationship**: Positive rapport maintained
- **Clarity**: Clear understanding of total compensation
- **Timeline**: Reasonable timeframe for decision

### Alternative Success Scenarios
- **Lateral move**: Same compensation but better growth opportunities
- **Future potential**: Clear path for advancement and raises
- **Cultural fit**: Strong alignment with company values and mission

## Post-Negotiation Actions

### If You Accept
1. **Written confirmation**: Email accepting the offer
2. **Documentation**: Get all terms in writing
3. **Transition planning**: Coordinate start date and onboarding
4. **Relationship building**: Connect with future colleagues

### If You Decline
1. **Professional response**: Thank them for the opportunity
2. **Clear reasoning**: Explain your decision respectfully
3. **Future consideration**: Leave door open for future opportunities
4. **Network maintenance**: Stay connected on professional platforms

Remember: The goal is to maximize your compensation while building a strong foundation for your future relationship with Square. Approach this as a collaborative discussion about mutual value creation.
